How it was built

Objectives: build a dataset and compare ML models that predict non-compliance, explain what drives the predictions, and deliver risk scores in a dashboard.

Read the reportFSSAI Pan India Jaggery Surveillance 2022.
Rebuild the rows3,060 samples, with every published total preserved.
PrepareEncode inputs, stratified split, fixed seed 42. Leakage columns removed.
TrainLogistic regression, tree, forest, boosting, XGBoost, SVM.
EvaluateAccuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, confusion matrix.
ExplainSHAP, permutation importance, input ablation.
Score and shareLow, medium and high risk bands in this dashboard.
Source: FSSAI, Pan India Jaggery Surveillance 2022. Built by Shadab Anwar (123FP0854), Food Process Engineering, NIT Rourkela. The calculator uses the selected XGBoost model’s precomputed predictions.
